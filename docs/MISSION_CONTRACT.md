# Mission Contract

A mission contains:

- objective
- constraints
- mandatory acceptance criteria
- optional acceptance criteria
- observations
- actions
- evidence

## Rules

1. Claims without evidence are not verified.
2. Repeating the same failed action without new evidence is forbidden.
3. Plans may change; the objective does not silently change.
4. External blockers are recorded explicitly.
5. Unsafe actions are delegated to a permission boundary.
6. Completion is a proof state, not a confidence state.
